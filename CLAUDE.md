## What it is
**Triviela** (trivia + trivela, the outside-of-the-foot curling kick, Morg's coinage on Oct 15, 2026. One L. Portuguese spells the kick "trivela" and has no "triviela" variant, so the extra i is ours and deliberate, said trih-vee-EL-uh) is a public, standalone trivia game about the beautiful game: a daily puzzle in an arcade-cabinet look. It began as a word game for Morg's daughter's youth team (Seattle Celtic G14) and grew into its own thing on Oct 16, 2026. It's a standalone game first. Morg may still embed it in Carpool HQ's Arcade later, for fun. Built by Morg (owner, names everything, approves all player-facing text) with Claude. Domain: triviela.net, live since Oct 17, 2026 (registered at Squarespace, its nameservers on Cloudflare pointing it and www at the Pages project, the registration to move to Cloudflare after the 60-day rule). The old address https://fernforager.github.io/blank-space-baby/ still works too, with its own saved progress. The repo, that address and the saved-progress keys (`bsb-…`) keep the old name Blank Space Baby on purpose.

**The lane (Oct 16, 2026):** trivia for the whole soccer family: the men's and the women's game, players and parents, US and global. Fun as trivia and fun as learning. Most daily games in the sport are guess-the-player puzzles for hardcore fans of men's European football. Triviela teaches (every card has a means line), covers rules, lingo, history and every league, and starts from the elementary basics: the offside rule, formations, field markings, ref signals, how many players, what a throw-in is, the positions. Soccer first: be stingy with non-soccer categories (Kid Slang is the model of a good one, its sentences all about soccer). **Design compass:** think Zach Gage. Simple rules, real stakes, a clean ending, a perfect day that's possible. No jokers, no second chances, no endless mode, no timer (a clock would punish reading the means lines, where the game teaches). Less is more: no instructions, labels or congrats text where icons, color and layout can carry it.

**In brief** (the whole spec is `GAME.md`): nine cards a day, three green (easy), three yellow, three red (hardest), each color its own column, and you play all nine. The score is the count of right answers, all nine is **GOLAZO!**, and a miss never ends the day. One board a day, the same for everyone, seeded by puzzle number and reset at local midnight. Right now the daily loops Morg’s own nine (`LOOP = 1`, board #1 pinned in `build/boards.json`), and every number carries a β for the playtest. The phone is an arcade cabinet: a dot-matrix sign, a CRT screen with three balls that fill as you answer, and cream keys. The share is a text grid of ⚽ and ❌ under 🟩🟨🟥, plus a square picture of the board.

## How to build and check
```
python3 build/build.py               # checks every card and string, applies the vetting, writes docs/index.html
python3 build/build.py --with-pending FILE   # a test copy where cards still in vetting play too (try a reworded card at 320×568)
python3 tools/vet.py status          # where every card stands: approved, locked, pending, flagged, out
python3 tools/checkdeck.py build/decks/mls.json   # one deck against the house style and the spec, without a build
python3 tools/screens.py             # every daily screen at three phone sizes, into out/
python3 tools/sweep.py 30 320 568    # plays every card of 30 days, flags overflow and scrolling (fails on a board that plays)
python3 tools/drawcheck.py           # a year of draws: guardrails, repeat spacing, same boards on every load, every wrong answer one the reviewers saw
python3 tools/lifecycle.py           # saving, resuming, locking, the share text, midnight, an offline result, a late day, real phone taps: the daily, one fixed board and the demo
python3 tools/textcheck.py           # every string in its slot on every screen at three sizes: fits, lines, even, centered, glyphs, plus out/text-sheet.png
python3 tools/textcheck.py --pseudo  # the same with every string about 40% longer and accented: tight spots, and English left in the code
python3 tools/tilecheck.py           # every card's category and hook in a real tile at three sizes (out/tilecheck.txt), exits 1 if a playing card fails
python3 tools/golive.py              # before main moves: the live build against the new one, date by date
python3 tools/livecheck.py           # after: plays today’s board on triviela.net as a new phone
python3 tools/curate.py export DIR [CATS]   # the Board Room’s pool.json and draft.json (parked)
python3 tools/library.py             # the library: the pools’ sizes and the consistency report (report --all lists every finding)
python3 tools/library.py check       # cards waiting on the reviewers against the library’s clear rules: spellings and own categories
python3 tools/library.py export DIR  # library.json for the Card Room, and the ledger’s dates (commit build/library/ledger.json)
```

### House style for all text (strict)
- **Never soccer or football (Morg, Oct 26, 2026):** to keep the game global, nothing the game shows, says or sends uses either word: the sign, labels, the share and its picture, the link preview, the Home Screen name, spoken labels. Icons, emoji and art carry the sport, and “the beautiful game” names it (the slogan exists for exactly this reason). Cards try to avoid both words too, keeping one only where the word is the fact (soccer mom, Soccer City, total football, footy). Internal names (deck ids, code, these notes) don’t reach players and can stay. The cards that said one in passing were reworded on Oct 30, 2026.
- One plain sentence per card, deadpan school-workbook tone. The answer supplies the fun.
- No period at the end (!, ?, ?! and … are fine). **Never semicolons, anywhere.** Use commas, colons, em dashes (closed up: word—word) or periods.
- The serial comma in every series of three or more (Morg’s pick, Oct 31, 2026, hub decision `serialcomma`): shirts, shorts, and socks. The 16 cards that went without it were reworded the same day and went back to the reviewers.
- Curly quotes and apostrophes only. Scores and ranges use an en dash (6–0, 2025–26). American spelling, Merriam-Webster’s (kickoff, halftime, shin guard, Euro not EURO).
- Punctuation never wraps away from the word or blank it touches. Hyphenated words and ranges never break across lines (the code handles both).
- Slang and lingo decks (Kid Slang, Parent Slang, Soccer Lingo: `alpha`, `parents`, `soccer`, `lingo-more`): every sentence is about soccer. No proper names, real or made up. Use roles (the coach, the ref, the keeper, the striker, the captain) and Mom, Dad, Grandpa, Grandma.
- The game’s two words for its 3×3 are grid and board (Morg, Oct 28, 2026): the word scoreboard is gone from every label.
- Trivia decks: plain factual sentences, real names allowed. Clubs and national teams take British plural verbs (“Arsenal are”, “Aston Villa have”), Morg's call on Oct 16, 2026.
- Kid-safe: anything borderline (sexual, drugs, alcohol, slurs, mean-spirited, private-life gossip) is out. When unsure, cut it.
- No song lyrics or quoted lines from songs, books or films, not even fragments.
