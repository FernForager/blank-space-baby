FONTLOG for the Triviela fonts
==============================

The two fonts in this folder are Modified Versions of fonts under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. Each is
renamed, as the license asks, and each carries its original copyright notice and license (OFL-*.txt here). They are
built by build/font/pixelfont.py in the Triviela repository.

Triviela Pixel (triviela-pixel-v1.woff2)
----------------------------------------
Based on Pixelify Sans, Copyright 2021 The Pixelify Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/eifetx/Pixelify-Sans).
License: OFL-TrivielaPixel.txt.

Pixelify Sans is drawn on a grid of 11 pixels to the em, but its outlines overhang each pixel by about a tenth, its
advances carry 0.44 px of padding, its kerning and line metrics fall between pixels, and its accents and punctuation are
drawn in half pixels, so it is never quite sharp on screen. Changes, Oct 19, 2026:

- Rebuilt on its own grid: 1100 units to the em, every pixel exactly 100 units. Letters and digits are read back from
  the static weight-400 master, one pixel per grid cell, a pixel on where the outline covers over half of the cell.
- Punctuation, symbols and accents redrawn by hand on whole pixels (quotes, apostrophes, dashes, brackets, the
  ellipsis, the accents and others). Accented letters are rebuilt from the base letter and the redrawn accent.
- Whole-pixel advances: one pixel between letters, four between words. Kerning removed. No ligatures.
- The lowercase j gets a dot (Pixelify's has none).
- Ascent 10 px, descent 3 px, line gap 0, all on the grid.
- One weight (Regular). Subset to Basic Latin, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A, Romanian comma letters and the common
  punctuation. Left out: the fractions, the broken bar, the soft hyphen, ĳ, kra, long s and ŉ.

Triviela Arcade (triviela-arcade-v1.woff2)
------------------------------------------
Based on Press Start 2P, Copyright 2012 The Press Start 2P Project Authors (cody@zone38.net), with Reserved Font
Name "Press Start 2P". License: OFL-TrivielaArcade.txt.

Changes, Oct 19, 2026: subset to Basic Latin, Latin-1 and a few punctuation marks, hinting and layout tables removed,
renamed. The outlines are unchanged.
