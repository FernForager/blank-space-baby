## The 2026 Ballon d’Or (London, Oct 26)

His senior clubs: Sporting in Lisbon (from 2002), Manchester United (2003 to 2009), Real Madrid (2009 to 2018), Juventus (2018 to 2021, 101 goals in 133 games), Manchester United again (2021 to November 2022, 145 goals in 346 games over both spells) and Al Nassr, who signed him in late December 2022. Five senior clubs, United twice. Nacional named its youth pitches the Cristiano Ronaldo Campus Futebol in 2007.

### 22. Ronaldo’s first red card for Portugal
In his record 226th game for Portugal, on Nov 13, 2025, Ronaldo got the first red card of his international career, sent off just after the hour for an elbow on Dara O’Shea off the ball, as the Republic of Ireland beat Portugal 2–0 in a World Cup qualifier (Troy Parrott scored both). Suspended, he missed the 9–1 win over Armenia that sent Portugal to the World Cup. He finished that qualifying campaign with 41 goals in 52 qualifiers, a record.
- **Proof:** fifa.com, Nov 19, 2025, https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/uefas-world-cup-26-qualifying-in-stats (the 226th cap, the first international red card, the 2–0, O’Shea, the 41 in 52), and fifa.com, Nov 13, 2025, https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/uefa-qualifiers-matchday-9-november-13 (Parrott’s goals, the red just after the hour), and fifa.com, Nov 16, 2025 (the suspension, per the helper’s read, the page not reread).
