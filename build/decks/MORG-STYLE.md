# Morg’s style: his edits, before and after

Morg wants future cards written closer to his own wording (Oct 31, 2026: “I’m also hoping the board room can learn from my edits also and align closer in future writing”). This file is how: every card he rewords in the Board Room, the beta’s text editor or the chat lands here as a before-and-after pair, and the patterns they show are drawn up at the top. Anyone who writes or rewords a card reads this file after `SPEC.md` (the deck writers, the editor and the desk), and the editor holds new wording to it. His wording is the model, never the facts: every edited card still gets the fact checker’s facts-only pass.

How it grows: when Morg marks boards ready (the hub’s “My boards are ready”), the GM adds one row per edited field below, then rereads the whole log and updates the patterns. A pattern needs at least two edits behind it, and each one names its examples by card id.
