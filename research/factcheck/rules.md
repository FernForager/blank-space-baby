# rules fact-check (2026-10-16)

65 cards checked. 61 pass. 4 have problems.

Source: theifab.com only: the 2026/27 Laws of the Game at https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/ (each Law page, the glossary, the VAR protocol and the temporary dismissals guidelines), the 2026/27 Law changes at https://www.theifab.com/law-changes/latest/, the 2025/26 Law changes at https://www.theifab.com/law-changes/2025-26/, and the IFAB news release confirming the 2026/27 Laws took effect July 1, 2026

- **[24]** `5 feet`: the fact is right (Law 1.8: a flagpost at least 1.5 m, 5 ft, high), but a flagpost’s minimum height is obscure trivia, not something a real fan knows, so d=3 doesn’t fit. Source: https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-field-of-play/. Fix: cut (the measure kind keeps 5 cards)
- **[54]** `jewelry`: no longer true. The 2026/27 Laws deleted “All items of jewellery … are forbidden and must be removed. Using tape to cover jewellery is not permitted” and now say accessories are allowed if they aren’t dangerous and are safely and securely covered (Law 4.1, and its FAQs on earrings and a necklace). The means line is wrong too. Source: https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-players-equipment/ and https://www.theifab.com/law-changes/latest/. Fix: new ex “Since 2026, jewelry that isn’t dangerous may be worn if securely covered”, new means “Dangerous items must still come off”, new hook “covered”, d=2, `"current": true`, and a groups entry ["jewelry", "shin guards"] (shin guards must be covered by the socks, so they would read as true in the blank)
- **[62]** `coin toss`: minor. The means line (“The winner picks a goal or the kickoff”) only repeats the sentence and teaches nothing. Source: https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-start-and-restart-of-play/ (Law 8 FAQ: “The coin must be tossed by the referee”). Fix: new means “Only the referee may toss the coin”
- **[64]** `shoot-out`: the means line is out of date. The Laws no longer call it “kicks from the penalty mark”: Law 10.3 is now “Penalties (penalty shoot-out)” and the glossary marks the old name as “Previously ‘Kicks from the penalty mark’”. Source: https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/glossary/football-terms/ and https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/determining-the-outcome-of-a-match/. Fix: new means “Once called kicks from the penalty mark”

Also noted, not counted as problems:
- theifab.com’s “latest” Law pages carry both the deleted 2025/26 wording and the added 2026/27 wording in their HTML, so a plain read shows both as if current. Only the added text was checked against. Confirmed by hand on Law 4: the jewelry ban is marked deleted and “Accessories are permitted as long as they are not dangerous and are safely and securely covered” is marked added
- The basics deck has jewelry cards too (basics 4, 114 and 145), so that deck’s check covers them
- Many cards turn on a minimum or a maximum (“at least 10 yards”, “at most 11”). Read very literally, a looser wrong answer is also true (a flagpost at least 5 feet tall is also at least 1 yard tall). That is the normal quiz reading of a limit, so those cards weren’t flagged. Rewording them as “the minimum is…” would make them airtight
