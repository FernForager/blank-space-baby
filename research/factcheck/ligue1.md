# ligue1 fact-check (2026-10-16)

61 cards checked. 60 pass. 1 has problems.

Source: ligue1.com only (English and French article pages, 2023–2026, including the 2026–27 club previews, the 2026–27 calendar, the champions roll and the September 2026 Classique numbers page)

- **[45]** `Breton derby`: ligue1.com never calls Rennes vs. Nantes the “fiercest” Breton derby. It says Breton derbies “are always spicy affairs” and that this is “never more true” than for Nantes vs. Rennes. The means line “Brittany’s spiciest rivalry” also leans on Nantes being in Brittany, which the page doesn’t state (it lists Nantes, Brest, Lorient and Angers among the clubs in Breton derbies). Source: https://ligue1.com/en/articles/l1_article_2365-stade-rennais-vs-fc-nantes-5-legendary-matches. Fix: ex “The spiciest Breton derby of all pits Rennes against Nantes”, means “Brest and Lorient play them too”

Also noted, not counted as problems:
- Nantes were relegated and Saint-Étienne lost their play-off, so both are in Ligue 2 in 2026–27. Their stadium, nickname and derby cards stay true because none says “Ligue 1”, but a card that calls them Ligue 1 clubs now would be wrong
- ligue1.com disagrees with itself on Albert Batteux: one page gives him 5 titles with Reims and 3 with Saint-Étienne (8, the card’s number), another gives 4 with Saint-Étienne. The card cites the page that says 8. Worth a look before Morg approves it
- The English for Les Dogues (the Mastiffs) and Les Aiglons (the Eaglets) is correct French but no ligue1.com page gives it
- The hook “PSG” is on six cards (20, 23, 27, 48, 49, 54)
