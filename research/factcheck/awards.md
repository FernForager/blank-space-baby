# awards fact-check (2026-10-16)

73 cards checked. 64 pass. 9 have problems.

Source: fifa.com and inside.fifa.com only (ballondor.com blocks direct fetches with a Cloudflare 403, so it was read through searches limited to ballondor.com, and no card depends on it)

- **[17]** `Alexia Putellas`: the sentence is right (2021 and 2022), but the means is wrong. Cristiano Ronaldo won The Best Men’s Player in 2016 and 2017, so Alexia isn’t The Best’s first back-to-back winner. She is the first in the women’s award. Source: https://www.fifa.com/en/the-best-fifa-football-awards/2025/articles/all-award-winners-history. Fix: new means “First to win it two years running”
- **[29]** `Luis Enrique`: facts right, but the means uses “its” for a club, against the British plural house style. Source: https://www.fifa.com/en/the-best-fifa-football-awards/2025/articles/all-winners-dembele-bonmati-luis-enrique-wiegman. Fix: new means “PSG won their first Champions League”
- **[45]** `Argentina`: true (3–3, then 4–2 on penalties at Lusail), but the src, the Messi Golden Ball article, never names France or the shootout. Source: https://www.fifa.com/match-centre/match/17/255711/285077/400128145?competitionEntryId=18. Fix: new src https://www.fifa.com/en/articles/world-cup-2022-qatar-france-mbappe-argentina-return
- **[54]** `2022`: true, but the src, the Messi Golden Ball article, doesn’t mention Emiliano Martínez or his Golden Glove. Source: https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/unai-simon-adidas-golden-glove (its list of past winners gives 2022: Emiliano Martinez). Fix: new src, that URL
- **[55]** `22`: true and current (Mbappé 4 + 8 + 10 = 22, past Klose’s 16, and Messi is on 21), but d=1 is a clear misrating. A brand-new soccer parent or a 10-year-old wouldn’t know the exact total, and the other buttons are 13, 8, 11 and 10. - **[60]** `7`: true (seven clean sheets in eight games, the most at one World Cup, past the old mark of five), but the src, the tournament review, only says Spain conceded one goal and never mentions the record. - **[70]** `Marta Award`: the win is right, but could not confirm “scorpion kick” on fifa.com. FIFA calls it a volley struck with her heel, back to goal, twisting in mid-air (an “acrobatic effort”), never a scorpion kick. Source: https://www.fifa.com/en/the-best-fifa-football-awards/2025/articles/lizbeth-ovalle-marta-winner. Fix: “Lizbeth Ovalle’s back-heel volley won the 2025 FIFA Marta Award”
