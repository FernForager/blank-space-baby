# soccer fact-check (2026-10-16)

55 cards checked. 48 pass. 7 have problems.

Source: merriam-webster.com, dictionary.com (including its Collins British entries)

- **[1]** `scissor kick`: neither dictionary has “scissor kick.” Dictionary.com sends it to scissors kick as a misspelling (Collins, football sense: leaps into the air, raises one leg and brings up the other to kick the ball), and merriam-webster.com gives a 404 for scissor kick (its scissors kick entry is only the swimming kick). The src redirects. Source: https://www.dictionary.com/browse/scissors-kick. Fix: w “scissors kick”, ex “She switched legs in midair and scored with a scissors kick”, src https://www.dictionary.com/browse/scissors-kick, and rename it in the volley groups entry
- **[2]** `volley`: wrong answers fit. “The defender kicked a cross out of the air before it could bounce” (or a chip, or a header) reads as an ordinary clearance of the other team’s ball. Source: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/volley. Fix: ex “To hit a volley, kick the ball out of the air before it bounces”, hook “bounces”
- **[26]** `midfielder`: fullback also fits “runs between both penalty areas all game,” and card [27] itself has the fullback running up from the back line to attack. Source: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/midfielder. Fix: ex “As a midfielder, Mom plays behind the forwards and ahead of the defense”
- **[27]** `fullback`: the means “Defender who plays out wide” is not the dictionary sense. M-W has a defensive player stationed nearest the defended goal, Collins one of two defensive players in front of the goalkeeper, and neither says wide. It also shares the hook “sideline” with [6]. Source: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/fullback. Fix: means “A defender near your own goal”, hook “sprinted”
- **[41]** `golden goal`: shares the hook “extra time” with [39] (and with lingo-more [46]). Source: https://www.dictionary.com/browse/golden-goal. Fix: hook “first goal”
- **[50]** `clinical`: the means “Precise and never wasteful” adds a sense neither dictionary gives. M-W (US, informal) has done with excellence and precision, and dictionary.com has dispassionately analytic. Source: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/clinical. Fix: means “Done with real skill and precision”
- **[53]** `gegenpress`: not a headword in either dictionary. Dictionary.com sends gegenpress to gegenpressing as a misspelling, and merriam-webster.com gives a 404. Source: https://www.dictionary.com/browse/gegenpressing. Fix: w “gegenpressing”, ex “After losing the ball, the team used gegenpressing to win it back”, src https://www.dictionary.com/browse/gegenpressing
