# nicknames fact-check (2026-10-16)

65 cards checked. 63 pass. 2 have problems.

Source: premierleague.com, laliga.com, bundesliga.com only (the deck uses no other league site, and every card’s club plays in the league whose site it cites)

- **[58]** `tidal wave`: the fact is right (laliga.com: “‘La Mareona’ (the tidal wave)”, for how many fans follow Sporting away), but d=2 is too low. It’s a Segunda club’s fan nickname with no English look-alike, the same kind of card as frogs, anchovies and basins, which are all d=3. Source: https://www.laliga.com/en-US/news/why-are-the-sporting-fans-called-la-mareona. Fix: d=3
- **[60]** `oak`: the fact is right (laliga.com: the Carbayón, an old oak on Uría Street, cut down in 1879), but d=2 is too low for the same reason: “Carbayones” gives no clue to “oak”, and the matching Spanish meaning cards (frogs, anchovies, basins, fava eaters) are d=3. Source: https://www.laliga.com/en-US/news/why-real-oviedo-is-known-as-the-carbayon-team. Fix: d=3

Also noted, not counted as problems:
