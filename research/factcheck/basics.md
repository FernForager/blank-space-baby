# basics fact-check (2026-10-16)

159 cards checked. 154 pass. 5 have problems.

Source: theifab.com Laws of the Game 2026/27 (the “latest” pages, read with the deleted old text stripped out, since those pages show struck-through 2025/26 wording next to the new text), merriam-webster.com

- **[1]** `shoe`: two wrong answers also fit. Cleats are soccer shoes (M-W: “shoes equipped with cleats”), and Law 4 gives the same ruling for a lost shin guard: “A player whose footwear or shinguard is lost accidentally … if before doing so the player plays the ball and/or scores a goal, the goal is awarded.” Source: https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-players-equipment/ and https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/cleat. Fix: add groups ["shoe", "cleats"] and ["shoe", "shin guard"]
- **[4]** `jewelry`: no longer true. The 2026/27 Law 4 deleted “All items of jewellery (necklaces, rings, bracelets, earrings, leather bands, rubber bands, etc.) are forbidden and must be removed.” It now reads “Accessories are permitted as long as they are not dangerous and are safely and securely covered. Dangerous items must be removed and not taped or covered.” A harmless rubber band may now stay on if it’s covered. A rewrite around “dangerous items must come off” doesn’t work either, because every other gear answer (glasses, gloves, armband) would fit too. Source: https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-players-equipment/. Fix: cut
- **[72]** `corner kick`: a wrong answer can be argued. “Players may not move the flagpost out of the way” sounds true at any restart (a throw-in or free kick near the corner). The Law only says it under corner kicks, but a player who picks throw-in has a fair complaint. Source: https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-corner-kick/. Fix: “At a corner kick, the ball sits in the small arc and the flagpost can’t be moved”
- **[114]** `yellow card`: the jewelry ban is gone in 2026/27 (see [4]). The caution now applies to unauthorized or dangerous equipment or accessories: “A player who refuses to comply or wears the item again must be cautioned.” Source: https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-players-equipment/. Fix: “A player who won’t remove a dangerous accessory earns a yellow card”, hook “accessory”, means unchanged (“Putting it back on later does too”)
- **[145]** `jewelry`: no longer true, and its means line contradicts its sentence. The 2026/27 Law 5.5 deleted “wearing jewellery or” from “Referees and other ‘on-field’ match officials are prohibited from … using any other electronic equipment”, and it now says “Accessories may be worn as long as they are not dangerous.” Source: https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-referee/. Fix: cut
