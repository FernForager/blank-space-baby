## The pile

**fifa.com, "Lionel Messi's World Cup records," Aug 31, 2026** (https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/lionel-messi-argentina-stats-records):
1. He retired from international play after the 2026 World Cup.
2. He finished with 21 World Cup goals. He passed Klose's record of 16 during the tournament and held the record until Mbappé scored twice in the bronze final to reach 22.
3. A record 23 World Cup wins (Klose had 17). The semifinal against England took him there.
4. Goals in nine straight World Cup games, a record (Just Fontaine and Jairzinho had six).
5. At 38 years and 357 days, the oldest World Cup hat-trick scorer, against Algeria in 2026. The old record was Cristiano Ronaldo's, at 33, against Spain in 2018.
6. Argentina's top World Cup scorer. Next: Batistuta (10), Maradona (8), Stábile (8), Kempes (6), Higuaín (5).
7. The top South American scorer in World Cup history, past Brazil's Ronaldo (15).
8. A record 7 World Cup goals from outside the box (Rivellino had 5).
9. Six World Cups, a record he shares with Cristiano Ronaldo.
10. A record 34 World Cup games, the first man to 30 (against Cabo Verde in the 2026 round of 32). Already a card: wc26:49.
11. A record 27 World Cup games as captain (Rafa Márquez 17, Maradona 16).
12. The most World Cup minutes: 3,064 (he passed Maldini's 2,217 in the 2022 final).
13. The only player with an assist at six World Cups.
14. A record 10 assists in World Cup knockout games (Pelé had 6).
15. The only man to score in the group stage, round of 16, quarterfinal, semifinal and final of one World Cup (2022).
16. The only player to score at a World Cup as a teenager, in his 20s and in his 30s (Pelé missed by four months).
17. His first and latest World Cup goals came 20 years and 21 days apart, a record.
18. A record 16 Player of the Match awards, five of them in 2026, a record for one World Cup. Already a card: wc26:38.
19. The only player with two Golden Balls, 2014 and 2022. Already a card: awards:0.

**inside.fifa.com** (https://inside.fifa.com/news/argentina-s-u-20-golden-boys, and https://www.fifa.com/news/world-cup-at-home-argentina-brazil-u20-world-cup-messi-3071371):
20. At the 2005 U-20 World Cup in the Netherlands he won the Golden Ball and the Golden Boot (six goals), and scored both goals, both penalties, as Argentina beat Nigeria 2–1 in the final. He was the second-youngest player in Argentina's squad.
21. His first senior World Cup goal came in a 6–0 win over Serbia and Montenegro in 2006.

**mlssoccer.com**
22. Player page (https://www.mlssoccer.com/players/lionel-messi/, read Oct 29, 2026): No. 10, forward, left-footed, 5′7″, 148 lbs, born June 24, 1987, in Rosario.
23. Campeones Cup (https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/lionel-messi-lifts-record-48th-trophy-as-inter-miami-win-campeones-cup, Sept 17, 2026): Inter Miami beat Cruz Azul 2–0 at Nu Stadium. Messi headed the first goal, his 100th for the club, and set up Casemiro's second. It was his record 48th trophy for club and country.
24. Same page: Inter Miami's trophies with him are the Leagues Cup (2023), the Supporters' Shield (2024), MLS Cup (2025, two assists in the 3–1 win over Vancouver) and the Campeones Cup (2026). The club had won nothing before he came. It also lists eight Ballon d'Or wins, 10 LaLiga titles and four Champions Leagues with Barcelona, two Ligue 1 titles with PSG, the 2022 World Cup and the 2021 and 2024 Copa Américas.
25. **Fresh, changes weekly:** the 2026 Golden Boot race (https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-golden-boot-tracker-2026-season-top-scorer, read Oct 29, 2026): Messi leads with 25 goals and 15 assists in 2,484 minutes, ahead of FC Dallas's Petar Musa (22). The regular season isn't over (a news report puts Decision Day on Nov 7, unconfirmed). Once it's decided, "Messi won the 2026 Golden Boot with ___ goals" is a fresh card that stays true. Back-to-back Golden Boots would also be new: he won 2025's with 29 (already a card: mls:54).

**intermiamicf.com** (https://www.intermiamicf.com/news/leo-messi-becomes-fifa-world-cuptm-s-all-time-leading-men-s-goalscorer, June 22, 2026 by its photo, no date on the page):
26. His World Cup goals by tournament before the 2026 knockouts: 1 in 2006, none in 2010, 4 in 2014, 1 in 2018, 7 in 2022, and 5 in his first two 2026 games (a hat trick against Algeria, two against Austria). The Austria pair, his 17th and 18th, made him the all-time leader.

**Fresh this week, for another category:** Harry Kane won the 2026 Ballon d'Or on Oct 26 at the London Palladium, the first Englishman since Michael Owen in 2001, and Alexia Putellas won the women's award for the third time (news reports, ESPN and NPR. ballondor.com is the Awards deck's source to confirm). So Messi stays on a record eight.
