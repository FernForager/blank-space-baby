## The pile

**uefa.com, "What UEFA records does Cristiano Ronaldo hold?", last updated Sept 27, 2026** (https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0253-0d820b46805f-b78ccae2c451-1000--what-uefa-records-does-cristiano-ronaldo-hold/):
1. The most international goals in world football: 146. **Grows:** watch list.
2. The most international games in world football: 234. **Grows.**
3. The first man to win the Champions League five times.
4. The most Champions League goals: 140, and the most games: 183. Already a card: ucl:18 (both records, no numbers).
5. The most goals in one Champions League season: 17, in 2013–14.
6. The most goals in Champions League knockout games: 67.
7. Champions League top scorer seven times: 2007–08, then six seasons in a row, 2012–13 to 2017–18.
8. The only player to score in three Champions League finals.
9. The only player to score in 11 straight Champions League games.
10. The most UEFA club competition goals (145) and games (197).
11. Euro records: six final tournaments, 30 games, 14 goals, 55 goals with qualifiers, goals at five Euros (2004, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020), and the only player with three goals at more than one Euro (2012, 2016, 2020).
12. The most World Cup qualifying goals: 41.
13. The first man to score ten hat tricks for his country.
14. The most goals at Euros and World Cups combined: 25, in a record 57 games.
15. UEFA's player of the year a record four times: 2008, 2014, 2016, 2017.

**fifa.com, "Cristiano Ronaldo the first man to score in six World Cups," June 23, 2026** (https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/cristiano-ronaldo-portugal-goal-record):
16. Two goals in Portugal's 5–0 win over Uzbekistan made him the first player to score at six World Cups. Messi had shared five with him. Already cards: wc26:14 and legends:3.
17. At 41 years and 138 days he became the second-oldest World Cup scorer, behind Roger Milla (42 years, 39 days).
18. Those goals took him past Eusébio (9) as Portugal's top World Cup scorer.
19. His World Cup hat trick came in a 3–3 draw with Spain in 2018, his best opponent (three goals).
20. Born Feb 5, 1985.

**fifa.com, the all-time scorers, July 19, 2026** (https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/fifa-world-cup-all-time-leading-scorers):
21. He finished with 11 World Cup goals: one each in 2006, 2010, 2014 and 2022, four in 2018 and three in 2026.

**fifa.com, the 2026 records page** (https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/records-broken-set):
22. Michael Laudrup, Messi and Ronaldo are each their country's youngest and oldest World Cup scorer.

**fifa.com, Portugal's team profile, June 3, 2026** (https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/portugal-team-profile-history):
23. He went to his sixth World Cup at 41 with 226 caps and 143 goals, both Portugal records (146 and 234 by September, fact 1).
24. In qualifying he became the outright top scorer in World Cup qualifiers, with two against Hungary.
25. Portugal won the 2025 Nations League under Roberto Martínez, the coach's first trophy with a national team.
