# The name search (Oct 17, 2026)

Morg wanted a catchier name, one that ties clearly to soccer or says it's a brain game. Nothing is picked yet, and the search is on hold. Triviela stays on the link (triviela.net) meanwhile, and the game shows no name on screen: the marquee reads “How well do you know the beautiful game?” instead (see CLAUDE.md, “The polish pass”).

## Where the ideas live
- **The name board:** https://claude.ai/artifact/EfRPR9kAg3VMgFfWaGRKhm, 2,329 names, each shown on a sign and in the share line, with Morg’s picks saved in the page’s database (collection `picks`, read it with ArtifactData). No picks yet as of Oct 17.
- **The lists behind it:** `research/naming/*.json` (`name`, `note`, `group`, `star`). a–d are goal-based (swapped into words, sounds like goal, brain and quiz, soccer and pop culture). e–h are made-up words (soccer plus trivia, puzzle and sports, sound play, global roots).

## What Morg said no to (don’t re-propose)
- **Nutmeg** (he just doesn’t love it), even though it led the first panel.
- **Anything with “touch”** (One Touch, First Touch): faintly creepy for a youth girls’ team.
- **Quizazo** and loud mashups like it.
- **Golden Goal, Know-It-Ball, Olé, One Shot, Kickoff, Onside, Sideline** were offered and not taken up.
- The **-le** ending (Kickle, Pitchle, Goaldle): worn out by Wordle copycats.

## What the panels said
- **Round 1** (the 18 players from `player-panel-2026-10-16-round2-voices.json`, asked about names): Nutmeg 5 favorites, The Daily Header 4 (but heading is banned for young US players), Golazo 3 (CBS runs a big Golazo brand), Kickoff came up independently from 4.
- **Round 2** (shown only the share text with the name blanked): nobody reached for goal puns. Two ideas dominated, the streak (The Run, Streak, Unbeaten, Racha) and one miss ends it (Sudden Death, Last Man, Last Ditch). Names that came up twice independently: One Touch, Sudden Death, Clean Sheet, Final Whistle, Kickoff, Sideline, Tiki-Taka. Everyone loved the ❌.

## Lessons
- The winning daily-game names are one short word you can say aloud and spell after hearing once. The web address matters little (Wordle began on a personal site).
- Triviela’s weak spot is that someone who hears it can’t spell it.
- Working backward from the share text helped more than lists did. If the search restarts, start from what the share line feels like, and check finalists against apps, trademarks and domains before Morg falls for one.
